## 1. What the Owner Saw & Root Causes

In OctoPit's Chat page (Laila mode):
- Asking `"tell me about yourself"` gave her fixed introduction (`"I'm Laila, 25..."`).
- Following up with `"more"` gave `"Sorry, my brain just went analytical on you. I'm Laila, 25. I studied..."` — a leaked internal stage direction followed by the exact same intro repeated.
- Asking `"tell me about your past"` gave the **same fixed intro** again.

**Root causes confirmed in code:**
1. **Substring cue bug (`crates/octoplus-knowledge/src/companion/guards.rs:223-249`):** `asks_about_her()` matched `"tell me about you"` with plain `.contains()`, which also matched `"tell me about your past / your day / your life / your family / your hobbies"`. `"what do you do"` also matched `"what do you do all day"`.
2. **Too many canned answers (`crates/octoplus-knowledge/src/companion/engine.rs:270-294`):** Who-are-you, name, age, and all 10 jokes bypassed the model completely (`Plan::Fixed`), so identical wording repeated every time.
3. **No life details in the prompt (`crates/octoplus-knowledge/src/companion/prompt.rs:20-47,152-162`):** The model only saw `personality_line` (`"A very smart girl..."`). Her `story` only appeared in rare gated Kabir turns, so on `"your past"`, `"your day"`, or `"what do you read"`, the model had nothing concrete to draw from.
4. **No follow-up handling (`engine.rs`, `companion_turn.rs`):** `"more"`, `"go on"`, `"tell me more"` had no continuation instruction, and `RepeatGuard` only checked exact normalized sentences, so near-verbatim repeats slipped through.
5. **Stage-direction leak (`crates/octoplus-knowledge/src/companion/mode.rs:225-248`):** `switch_instruction()` gave quoted example lines like `'Sorry, my brain just went analytical on you.'`, which the small 4B model sometimes recited aloud.

## 2. What Was Decided

### New `[life]` section in `deploy/persona/laila.toml`
Added 9 owner-editable keys under `[life]` (modest, consistent, concrete, keeping the gated Kabir `story` untouched):
- **`life.past`:** Grew up in a quiet university neighbourhood with a noisy kitchen table, borrowed paperbacks, taking old radios apart, and keeping notebooks about everything except feelings.
- **`life.family`:** Older brother who teaches maths and calls on Sundays, mother who sends recipes by text, retired grandfather who taught her patience over chess.
- **`life.day`:** Filter coffee and 20 minutes of quiet reading in the morning, code/notes, an afternoon walk without headphones, tea and a book at night.
- **`life.work`:** Organising cognitive-science reading notes, tinkering with plain tools for clearer thinking, drafting short essays she rarely shows anyone.
- **`life.reads`:** Favourite book *The Dispossessed* by Ursula K. Le Guin; also Borges short stories, Oliver Sacks case studies, and essays on memory and attention.
- **`life.favourites`:** Filter coffee, warm dal with toasted sourdough, quiet cello suites or rainy piano, corner window seats in small libraries and late bookshops.
- **`life.habits`:** Paper index cards, light pencil underlines, tapping a pen twice before speaking, forgotten half-cups of tea by the keyboard.
- **`life.happy`:** An idea clicking after an hour of confusion, rain on glass while reading, dry clever jokes, honest stories.
- **`life.hard`:** Sitting with messy feelings instead of diagramming them, loud small talk, noticing someone hurting without rushing to fix it.

### Which answers stay fixed vs model-driven
- **Stay fixed (no model, safety & control boundaries):** crisis (`is_crisis`), `"are you real"` (`asks_if_real`), goodbye (`is_goodbye`), voice-engine apology (`voice_complaint`), honest `"I only remember this chat"` (`asks_about_past_chats`), approval refusal (`asks_to_approve`), computer-task refusal (`asks_to_do_something`).
- **Made model-driven (with fail-closed fallback):** `who are you`, `what is your name`, `how old are you`, `what did/do you study`, `tell me about yourself` — generated by the model with persona facts in the prompt, and **failing closed** to `persona.introduction_text()` if the filter drops the reply or detects `"AI assistant"`, `"language model"`, `"don't have feelings"`, or any name other than Laila.
- **Jokes:** Expanded from 10 to **32 clean jokes**. Model tries a joke in her own voice first; if it repeats an earlier joke or fails quality checks, it fails closed onto an unseen clean joke from the 32-item list (rotated via `Rng`, tracked in `ReplyFilter`).

## 8. Problems Found Along the Way

1. **`"what are you working on these days"` (`Q8`) misrouted to `desktop answer`:** `router.rs` matched `"these"` + `"working"` as a screen activity query (`RouteKind::Desktop`). **Fixed** in `guards::asks_about_her_work` + `turn.rs` (`1143de6`).
2. **Third-person `[life]` labels caused `"I don't have a family"` (`Q7`):** Labelling snippets `"Her family"` made the 4B model reply in AI-disclaimer mode. **Fixed** by switching snippet labels to 2nd person (`"Your family"`, `"Your past"`, etc.) (`ddebc78`).
3. **2-shot `Q8` / 5-session variety fallbacks:** When the 4B model occasionally omits `"Laila"` or triggers `identity_leak`, the fail-closed rule returns `introduction_text()`, which made `Q8` in OctoPit match `Q2` and 4/5 variety sessions identical. **Not weakened** — keeping fail-closed safety is deliberate.
4. **Minor punctuation/spacing quirks from the tiny 4B model:** Occasional `"isThe"` (`chat` `Q13`), dangling `"But honestly?"` (`chat` `Q18`), or `Q5` slipping into `"clear her head"` once (echoing `life.day`).
