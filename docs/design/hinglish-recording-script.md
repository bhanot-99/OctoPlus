# Hinglish recording script (Phase 12, C-S10)

Read each line once, naturally, at your normal speed, 16 kHz mono WAV, one file per group. 78 lines in 8 groups, about 15 minutes; then 10 to 15 minutes of free talk (tell her about your day in Hinglish).

Do not read private things: where the lines mention a password or phone number they only say you will NOT share it.


## 1. Everyday talk (mixed)

1. Aaj ka din bahut lamba tha, thoda thak gaya hoon.
2. Kal subah meeting hai, mujhe jaldi uthna padega.
3. Chai peene ka mann ho raha hai, tum bhi piyogi?
4. Arre yaar, wo file kahan chali gayi?
5. Mujhe lagta hai ye plan thik hai.
6. Weekend pe kya kar rahe ho?
7. Abhi thoda busy hoon, baad mein baat karte hain.
8. Kal raat ko movie dekhi, kaafi achhi thi.
9. Mera laptop aaj bahut slow chal raha hai.
10. Dinner mein kya banana hai aaj?

## 2. Feelings

1. Aaj main thoda nervous feel kar raha hoon.
2. Mujhe bahut khushi hui jab pata chala.
3. Kabhi kabhi akela feel hota hai, but it's okay.
4. I am really proud of this project, sach mein.
5. Thoda stress hai, deadline paas aa rahi hai.
6. Bas aaj ka din achha nikal jaye, itna chahiye.
7. Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha kya karna chahiye.
8. Tumse baat karke achha lagta hai.
9. Neend nahi aa rahi, dimaag mein bahut kuch chal raha hai.
10. Aaj mood kuch off hai.

## 3. Plans and times

1. Thursday ko mera interview hai subah das baje.
2. Next week main ghar jaa raha hoon.
3. Kal shaam ko gym jaunga.
4. Teen baje meeting hai, usse pehle lunch kar lunga.
5. Is mahine ke end tak report submit karni hai.
6. Parso mera exam hai, thoda padh lena chahiye.
7. Tomorrow morning flight hai, packing baaki hai.
8. Saturday ko dosto ke saath bahar jaana hai.
9. Aaj raat ko jaldi sona hai.
10. Kab tak ye kaam khatam hoga?

## 4. People and things

1. Meri behen Berlin shift ho gayi hai.
2. Mera dog Rex vacuum cleaner se darta hai.
3. Papa ne aaj phone kiya tha.
4. Mere boss ne schedule phir se change kar diya.
5. Meri girlfriend ko pasta bahut pasand hai.
6. Mere dost ka birthday kal hai.
7. Maa ne aaj paratha banaya tha.
8. Mera naya phone bahut fast hai.
9. Wo restaurant bahut loud tha, mujhe pasand nahi aaya.
10. Mere paas ek purani guitar hai.

## 5. Questions to her

1. Tum kaisi ho aaj?
2. Kya tum mujhe ek joke suna sakti ho?
3. Mera naam yaad hai tumhe?
4. Aaj ka weather kaisa hai?
5. Tumhe kya pasand hai, music ya silence?
6. Kuch mazedaar batao na.
7. Kya tum real ho ya computer program?
8. Aaj maine kya kaha tha tumse?
9. Mujhe kuch recommend karo dekhne ke liye.
10. Tum kya soch rahi ho abhi?

## 6. Controls

1. Abhi nahi, baad mein baat karte hain.
2. Ek ghante ke liye chup raho please.
3. Thoda kam baat karo.
4. Thoda zyada baat karo.
5. Ye joke achha tha, haha.
6. Ye joke funny nahi tha.
7. Yaad rakho ki mujhe chai pasand hai.
8. Wo bhool jao jo maine abhi kaha.
9. Mere ghar mein koi aaya hai, guest mode on karo.
10. Bye, kal milte hain.

## 7. Numbers, names, English words inside Hindi

1. Mera phone number nahi dunga, wo private hai.
2. Do hazaar bees ka budget hai is saal.
3. Zoom link bhej dena please, meeting ke liye.
4. WhatsApp pe message kar diya hai maine.
5. Laptop ki battery sirf do ghante chalti hai.
6. Mera password hint blue hai, par password nahi bataunga.
7. Pune se Bangalore ki flight kitne baje ki hai?
8. Monday se Friday office hai, Saturday off.
9. Is project ka deadline fifteen December hai.
10. Amazon se kuch order kiya hai, kal aayega.

## 8. Longer speech (read naturally)

1. Aaj subah main jaldi utha, chai banayi aur thodi der balcony mein baitha. Bahut shaant tha sab kuch, aur mujhe laga ki kabhi kabhi bas aise hi baithna chahiye.
2. Kal mere interview mein thoda nervous tha, par jab baat shuru hui to sab theek ho gaya. Ab bas result ka wait hai, aur main try kar raha hoon ki zyada soch na karoon.
3. Mujhe lagta hai ki hum sab kabhi kabhi apne aap ko samjha nahi paate, aur phir kisi se baat karke achha lagta hai, chahe wo ek computer hi kyun na ho.
4. Weekend pe plan hai ki pehle ghar saaf karunga, phir dost ke saath lunch, aur shaam ko ek achhi si movie dekhunga agar neend nahi aayi to.
5. Meri behen ne kaha ki Berlin mein sardi bahut hoti hai, par wahan ka kaam achha hai, to wo khush hai, aur main bhi uske liye khush hoon.
6. Kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki main bahut zyada analyse karta hoon har cheez ko, aur feelings ko bas mehsoos karna bhool jaata hoon.
7. Mujhe chai bina sugar ke pasand hai, aur subah ki chai sabse achhi lagti hai jab bahar halki baarish ho rahi ho.
8. Chalo ab main kaam pe lagta hoon, tumse baad mein baat karta hoon, aur dhanyawaad sunne ke liye.
